At the Court's private conferences the justices took notes on printed forms, one box for each colleague's remarks. These pages come from the papers of Justices Brennan, Blackmun and Powell, with a few of Marshall's and Rehnquist's.

Where the labels come from

Case names, terms, note-takers and box labels are Zooniverse's records of the SCOTUS Notes project, checked against the archive's own file names and, where a page prints its case, against the page itself. 73 pages and 20 boxes carry a label the scan contradicts, mostly because Zooniverse looked a case up by its docket number in the wrong term: Brennan's notes on Roe v. Wade, for instance, are filed there as In re Stolar. These are shown with the label the scan supports, and a note on each says what Zooniverse has and how we know; search for “corrected” to list them. Where Zooniverse records no note-taker, it comes from the initials in the archive's file name. The pages of one case are shown together, in page order.
